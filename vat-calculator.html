VAT calculator

Add VAT to a net price or strip VAT from a gross price. Includes a current Israel 18% preset and other common rates.

How to use: enter an amount, choose add or remove VAT, and pick a rate or a country preset. Net, tax, and gross update as you type.

VAT formulas

To add VAT: gross = net × (1 + rate). To remove VAT: net = gross ÷ (1 + rate). The Israel preset uses 18%, the standard rate in force from 1 January 2025. Always confirm the current official rate before you issue an invoice.

Questions

Is Israel VAT still 18%?

The standard rate moved to 18% on 1 January 2025. This tool does not update itself if the law changes, so check the Israel Tax Authority if you are unsure.

Does this handle reduced rates?

Enter any custom rate. Reduced and zero rates vary by country and product, so the presets are only starting points.
