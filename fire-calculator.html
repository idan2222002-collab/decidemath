FIRE / retirement calculator

Estimate the portfolio you need for financial independence and how long current savings and contributions might take to reach it.

How to use: enter annual spending, a withdrawal rate, current savings, monthly saving, and an expected return. The FIRE number and time estimate update as you type.

How the FIRE number is calculated

FIRE number = annual spending ÷ withdrawal rate. The popular 4% rule implies 25 times annual spending. Time to FIRE assumes monthly contributions and monthly compounding at a constant return, which is a planning estimate, not a guarantee.

Questions

What withdrawal rate should I use?

4% is a common starting point from U.S. historical studies. A lower rate is more conservative; a higher rate is more aggressive.

Does this include Social Security or a pension?

No. If you expect other income, lower the annual spending figure by that amount.
