Israel net salary calculator (English)

Estimate monthly take-home pay from an Israeli gross salary, using 2026 income-tax brackets, credit points, and employee National Insurance and health rates.

How to use: enter your monthly gross salary in shekels and the number of tax credit points on your payslip (2.25 is the usual base for an Israeli resident). The estimate updates as you type. Optional deductions such as pension can be typed in as a monthly amount.

How this 2026 estimate is built

Income tax uses the ITA monthly work-income brackets in the April 2026 deductions booklet: 10% up to ₪7,010, then 14%, 20%, 31%, 35%, and 47%. Credit points reduce that tax; the booklet lists one point as ₪242 a month in 2026. Employee National Insurance and health contributions use BTL rates from 1 January 2026: 1.04% and 3.23% up to ₪7,703, then 7% and 5.17% up to a ₪51,910 monthly cap. This is an educational estimate, not a payslip.

Questions

What are tax credit points?

They lower the income tax you owe. A typical Israeli resident starts with 2.25 points; extra points can apply for children, residency status, or other credits. Use the number on your payslip if you have one.

Does this include pension or travel?

Only if you type those amounts under other deductions. It does not automatically apply pension tax relief or travel allowances.

Is ₪242 still the credit-point value?

That is the monthly value in the ITA 2026 deductions booklet updated April 2026. If the ITA publishes a new figure, type it in the credit-point value field.
