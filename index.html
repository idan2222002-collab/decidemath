Questions

Is this really free?

Yes. The calculators are free to use and always will be. A tip link or affiliate links may be added later so the site can cover its own time.

Do you store my numbers?

No. There is no account and no backend. The formulas run in your browser.

Who is this for?

Anyone comparing a loan, Israeli tax, a salary, or an everyday percentage — especially English-speaking users who want a fast, no-signup tool.
