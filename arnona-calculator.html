Arnona calculator

Estimate Israeli municipal property tax (arnona) from floor area and the rate your city lists for your zone and property use.

How to use: find the ₪ per square metre rate on your city's arnona notice or website, enter your taxable floor area, and choose how many months you want to price. The annual and monthly figures appear immediately.

How arnona is estimated

This tool multiplies floor area by the annual ₪ per m² rate your municipality sets, then scales that annual amount by the number of months you enter. There is no single national arnona rate: cities, zones, building use, and sometimes discounts for new residents or empty properties all change the bill.

Questions

Where do I find my rate?

On your arnona bill or the municipality website. Rates differ for residential, commercial, and other uses, and often by neighbourhood zone.

Does this include discounts?

No. If you have a discount or exemption, lower the rate you type, or shorten the period, to match the reduced bill.
