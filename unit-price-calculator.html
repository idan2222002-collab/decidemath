Unit price calculator

See the price per unit of a product, and optionally compare two pack sizes to find the better deal.

How to use: enter the shelf price and the pack quantity for item A, using one consistent unit such as grams or litres. To compare, fill in item B the same way. The cheaper unit price is highlighted.

How unit price is compared

Unit price is price divided by quantity. The better deal is the lower unit price, as long as both quantities use the same unit. Convert first if one pack is labelled in ounces and the other in grams.

Questions

Does this include bulk discounts at checkout?

No. It only uses the prices and quantities you enter.

What if one pack is a different unit?

Convert both quantities to the same unit before you compare, or the cheaper-looking figure will be misleading.
