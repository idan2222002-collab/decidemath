Rent vs buy calculator

Compare the cash cost of renting with the net cost of buying a home over a period you choose.

How to use: fill in the home price, mortgage, rent, and holding costs, then set how many years to compare. Net cost of renting and buying updates as you type.

How rent vs buy is compared

Rent cost is the sum of monthly rent, grown each year. Buying cash-out is the down payment, closing costs, mortgage payments, tax, insurance, and maintenance. Net cost of buying subtracts remaining equity after selling costs. It does not credit tax deductions or an investment return on the down payment.

Questions

Why might renting win even if prices rise?

Closing costs, selling commissions, maintenance, and interest can outweigh modest appreciation, especially if you move after only a few years.

Should I include the return I could earn on the down payment?

This version does not. If you would invest that cash while renting, renting looks better than the headline net-cost gap.
