Salary to hourly calculator

Turn an annual salary into an hourly equivalent, using the weeks and hours you actually work.

How salary converts to an hourly rate

Hourly rate = annual salary ÷ (weeks × hours per week). A standard full-time year is 52 × 40 = 2,080 hours, so a $75,000 salary is about $36.06 an hour. The daily figure assumes a five-day week.

Questions

Should I use 2,080 hours?

Use 2,080 if you work 40 hours and 52 weeks. Reduce the weeks if you have unpaid leave.

Is this before or after tax?

It uses the salary figure you enter, with no tax withheld.
