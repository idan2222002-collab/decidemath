Tip calculator

Add a tip to a bill and split the total evenly between the people at the table.

How to use: type the bill total, the tip percent you want to leave, and how many people are splitting. The tip, the grand total, and the per-person shares update as you type.

How the tip is calculated

Tip amount = bill × (tip percent ÷ 100). The grand total is bill plus tip, then both the total and the tip can be divided by the number of people. It does not add sales tax or VAT automatically — include tax in the bill figure if it is already on the receipt.

Questions

Should I tip on the amount before or after tax?

Local custom varies. If the receipt already includes tax, many people tip on the total shown.

Can I use any currency?

Yes. The result is in the same units you type.
