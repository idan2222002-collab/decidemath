Cap rate calculator

See the capitalization rate of a rental from gross income, operating expenses, and value. Mortgage payments are left out of NOI on purpose.

Cap rate formula

Capitalization rate = NOI ÷ property value. NOI is gross rental income minus operating expenses such as tax, insurance, maintenance, management, and vacancy. Mortgage payments are financing costs, not operating expenses, so they stay out of this formula.

Questions

What is a good cap rate?

It depends on the market, asset type, and risk. Compare a property to similar nearby sales rather than chasing a single target number.

Why exclude the mortgage?

Cap rate is meant to compare the property itself, independent of how you finance it. Use cash-on-cash return to judge leveraged deals.
