Mas shevach calculator

A simple English estimate of Israeli real-estate betterment tax (mas shevach) from the gain on a sale.

How to use: enter the sale price, what you paid, selling and buying costs, and any inflation adjustment you have already calculated. The default rate is 25%, which you can change. Read the caveats — exemptions often reduce the real tax to far less than this sketch.

How this mas shevach sketch works

Estimated taxable gain is sale price minus purchase price, costs, and any inflation adjustment you enter. Tax is that gain times the rate (25% is a common starting point for individuals). Real assessments can be zero because of a residence exemption, the linear exemption, or official indexation rules. Use this only to understand the moving parts.

Questions

What is mas shevach?

It is Israel's betterment tax on the inflation-adjusted gain when you sell real estate, administered with the land-appreciation tax rules. It is separate from ordinary income tax.

Why might the real tax be zero?

Selling a qualifying residence, unused exemption amounts, or the linear method for older purchases can wipe out or shrink the tax. This calculator does not apply those reliefs automatically.
