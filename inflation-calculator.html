Inflation calculator

Project the future cost of today's prices and the purchasing power left in a fixed amount of money.

How to use: enter today's amount, an average inflation rate, and a number of years. Future cost and remaining purchasing power appear immediately.

Inflation formula

Future cost = amount × (1 + inflation)years. Purchasing power of the same nominal sum is amount ÷ (1 + inflation)years. The rate is an average; real inflation varies year to year.

Questions

Can I use a negative rate?

Yes. A negative rate models deflation, so future prices fall and purchasing power rises.

Is this official CPI?

No. You choose the rate. Look up your country's consumer-price index if you want a historical average.
