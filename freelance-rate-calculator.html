Turn the income you want, your expenses, and realistic billable time into a minimum hourly and day rate.

How to use: enter the income you want, expenses, billable weeks and hours, and a tax buffer. Hourly, day, and weekly rates update as you type.

How to set a freelance rate

Add business expenses to the income you want, then lift that total by a buffer for tax and unpaid admin. Divide by realistic billable hours — not every hour you work is billed. The 8-hour day rate is the hourly rate times 8, even if your week uses a different billable-hour count.

Questions

Why not just divide salary by 2,000 hours?

Because freelancers have unpaid holidays, admin, sales time, and expenses. Using only billable hours keeps the rate honest.

What buffer should I use?

Many freelancers start around 25–40% to cover tax and downtime, then adjust for their country and workload.
