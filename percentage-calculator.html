Percentage formulas

X% of Y is (X ÷ 100) × Y. “X is what percent of Y” is (X ÷ Y) × 100. Percentage change is (new − old) ÷ |old|. An increase or decrease applies 1 ± X% to Y.

Questions

Why is change undefined from 0?

Dividing by a starting value of zero is not a defined percentage change. Use a different starting number.

Is a 10% fall then a 10% rise a wash?

No. A 10% fall from 100 is 90, and a 10% rise from 90 is 99.
