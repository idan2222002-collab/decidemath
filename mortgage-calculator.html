Mortgage payment calculator

Estimate the monthly principal and interest on a fixed-rate loan, plus how much interest you would pay over the full term.

How the mortgage calculator works

The monthly payment uses the standard fixed-rate formula: payment = P × r(1+r)n ÷ ((1+r)n − 1), where r is the monthly rate and n is the number of months. If the rate is 0, the payment is simply the principal divided by n.

Questions

Does this include taxes and insurance?

No. It only estimates principal and interest. Escrow, PMI, and fees would raise the real monthly bill.

Can I use any currency?

Yes. Enter numbers in the currency you care about. The result uses the same units, with no conversion.
